# The sound proof of the Development game: the game's own demo director (Source/MurderInc/Flow/MIDemoDirector.cpp) plays
# everything a run can sound like, and with -LogCmds="LogMIAudioHost Verbose" the log names every file it starts
# ("play <file> sound=... category=... volume=... pitch=..."), so sound_table.py can ask the viewer's recording for
# each of them. On the box, with a package:
#   lag-test.sh run <name> game=dev demo=sound-demo.txt view=sound-watch.txt audio=1 join=3 probe=0.25 secs=330 -- "-LogCmds=LogMIAudioHost Verbose"
#   python3 sound_table.py /project/ue/cloud/lag/<name>
# In order: the home screen (title music and the street alone), the set-up screens (every press a ui_click, a part
# looked at a ui_hover), four guns (the CHOPPER, the NINE, the STICK with a silencer, the STRETCH: the two rifles of
# the pack fire with the CHOPPER's sounds), the run's start (radio, the run-start line), each gun's single rounds, a
# burst, the reload, the empty magazine and the dry press, the rifle butt, steps walking and running, a jump; two
# reels taken (the reel sound, its sting, a line), a fight on the avenue (the Goons' alert, their guns, the near
# misses, the hits, their deaths, the kill lines), the propane tanks (fuse and blast), the last reel by hand (the grab's
# rising tone), the truck (the last sting, "What's my name" and the crowd), the debrief. The player cannot die here
# (god 1): the hurt, the heartbeat and the death are sound-dead.txt.
