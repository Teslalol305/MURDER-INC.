# The sound proof with mouse and keyboard, for the Shipping game (played by rtcaudio.py):
#   lag-test.sh run <name> game=ship view=sound-keys.txt audio=1 join=3 probe=0.25 secs=200
# One kind of sound at a time, each under a "say SND <name>" line, so audio_find.py can be asked for that part of the
# recording (the times are in view.log and in sound.wav.json). What each part must hold (Content/Data/audio.json):
#   card, home       the title music (stem_*) and the street (bed_day) behind the click card and on the home screen
#   menu-moves       Down, Up: ui_hover each; hit-the-block, the Next buttons: ui_click each
#   briefing, run    the run starts: radio_in, a voice line (inc_*, 8 runs in 10), then street and music
#   selector         B: dry_fire at 1.6 times its pitch, quiet; three single rounds two seconds apart: chopper_shot,
#                    chopper_close_*, chopper_mech_*, chopper_tail_*, and the case on the ground half a second later
#                    (brass_asphalt_* or brass_concrete_*)
#   burst            half a second of automatic fire
#   reload           R: cloth, rifle_magout, rifle_magin, rifle_bolt, cloth
#   empty, dry       the magazine fired empty, then two more presses: dry_fire
#   nine             2: holster, draw; three rounds: nine_shot, nine_close_*, nine_mech_*, nine_tail_*; R: pistol_magout,
#                    pistol_magin, pistol_slide; 1: back to the CHOPPER
#   melee            F: whoosh (3.5 s after the switch back: the rules refuse the butt while a gun is being drawn)
#   walk, sprint     W: step_* (the surface under the feet); with Shift: step_*_run; Space: jump, then land
#   pause            P: the world goes silent, the music stays, thinner (45 %, behind 1.3 kHz)
#   pause-menu       Down three times (ui_hover), Enter on Settings (ui_click)
#   volume-down      on the Volume row: Left forty times, 80 % to 40 %: the music must fall by 6.0 dB
#   volume-up        Right forty times: back where it was
#   resume           Backspace closes Settings, P resumes: street and music again
