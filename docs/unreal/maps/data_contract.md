# Level file data contract (Canal Street and Midtown)

For the engine lane that writes `FMILevelData::ForLevel(Id)` (MAPS.md E1, E2, E7, E8, E10, E12). Written 2026-11-28 from
what `tools/levels/build_level.py` actually writes (`tools/levels/data/level_chinatown.json`, `level_midtown.json`,
generated by `tools/levels/layouts/*.py` through `levelkit.py`), and checked by `tools/levels/test_levels.py`, which
asserts the lists below against the generated files. Units: every position has a `web` form (metres, +Y up, north = -Z)
and a `ue` mirror (cm, X east, Y south, Z up); the loader reads `ue`. Drones are removed (owner, 2026-11-28): there is no
air point, no Buzzard setting and no doorway in either file.

## 1. Sections the Block's level.json also has (same schema, same loader code)

`meta`, `bounds`, `zones`, `places`, `ground`, `playerStart`, `objectives`, `extraction`, `enemySpawns`, `coverPoints`,
`explosives`, `interior`, `vantage`, `cinematicPath`, `steam`, `puddles`, `buildings`, `physics`, `colliders`,
`orientedBoxes`, `signage`, `layout`. Differences in content, not shape:

- `enemySpawns.points[]`: `type` is always `"ground"` (zero `air` points). Each point also has `layer` (string, id of a
  `nav.layers[]` entry) and `name` (`c1`, `g12`, `rf1` ...); an optional `overlooks` (array of zone ids) and `enabled`
  (bool, absent means true) and `enabledWhen` (text only). **The loader must skip a point with `enabled: false`** (E8).
  Canal's three (`rf1`..`rf3`, layer `roof`) were disabled until nav step A and are enabled since 2026-11-28; no level has one off now.
- `objectives[]` and `extraction` carry `layer` (string). `extraction.arrival` is `"afterAll"` on Midtown only (E10):
  the truck is hidden, its colliders (`extraction.truck.colliders`, indices into `colliders.boxes`, owner kind `truck`)
  stay out of the box world and the zone is inactive until every reel is secured, then a 5 s roll-in. Canal's truck is
  there from the start, as on the Block.
- `interior.doorways` is `[]` in both (they served only the Buzzards). `interior.boxes[]` keep `name`, `web`, `ue`, and
  `reel` (Canal's two reel rooms).
- `colliders.boxes[]` owner kinds: `level`, `prop` (index into `propPlacements.items`), `explosive` (index into
  `explosives.items`), `truck`. Flags `bulletOnly`, `playerOnly`, `noNav` as on the Block.
- `ground.surfaces` and `floors` are lists of rectangles, not the Block's sampled grid; `buildings`, `layout` are art data
  the engine can ignore.

## 2. Sections the Block's file has and these files do NOT have

`el` (E2: must become optional: `bHasDeck` false, no deck logic), `train`, `glazing`, `facades`, and **`nav` in the
Block's shape** (see 3). A loader that requires any of these cannot read the maps. `--el-fallback` on `build_level.py`
writes a far-away `el.deck` for a loader test before E2 lands.

## 3. New sections (absent from the Block's file; optional in the loader)

**`nav`** (object; same key name as the Block's nav grid, a different shape: the Block's has `cols/rows/rule/free/blocked/grid`
for one plane, the maps' is multi-layer, so key on presence of `layers`):
`cell` 0.5, `inflate` 0.5, `bandMin` 0.47, `bandMax` 1.5 (metres); `layers[]`: `{id, y, rect {minX,maxX,minZ,maxZ}, floor
("plane" | "boxes"), cols, rows, grid}`; **`grid` is `null`**, written by the engine bake (`-run=MILevelBake`, MAPS.md D2)
with FMIBoxWorld's code, never by Python. `floor: "plane"` = the street (y 0, the Block's rule); `"boxes"` = a roof layer
(walkable top of the colliders at that height, midtown.md 17.3). Canal: `street`, `roof` (y 5.7). Midtown: `street`, `r1` 14.0,
`r2` 17.6, `r3` 21.2. `links[]`: `{id, a, b (layer ids), kind "stair", path [[x,y,z]...] (metres, web), name?, usedFrom?}`.
`spawnReach`: object layer id -> array of layer ids a spawn on that layer may reach. `firingCellRange` (int, cells, 30),
`strandedAfter` (int, seconds, 10).

**`guide`** (object, G2/G4): `mode: "routeGraph"`; `graph.nodes[]` `{id, layer, pos [x,y,z]}`; `graph.edges[]` `{from, to}`
(node indices), `kind` `"stair"` | `"drop"`, `link` (link id) or `drop` (drop id), `path` (polyline), `bias` (extra metres
of cost; Midtown's service stair and drops 10), `oneWay` (drops, true: down only), `step {kind, link, height}`;
`targetHysteresis` 0.15, `pathHysteresis` 0.10 (Canal), `markerHeightLabelOver` 3.0 (Midtown), `intendedOrder` (reel ids),
`designLegs`, `waivers[]`, `alternatives[]` (Canal; checker inputs). Stairs both ways, drops down only.

**`drops`** `{source, items[]}`: `{id, from [x,y,z], to [x,y,z], height (m), what}`, Midtown also `fromLayer`, `toLayer`,
`walkOff` (polyline). Every drop height + 1.0 m jump apex is under 4.02 m (free whether walked or jumped).

**`tuning`** (E7), merged over config.json for this level only; every key optional:
`difficulty.maxAliveAdd` (int, Canal 1), `difficulty.pursuitBudgetAdd` (int, Midtown 2), `spawn.excludePlayerRoom` (bool,
both true), `rating.time` (Midtown `[[330,2],[480,1]]`: seconds, stars), `startPitch` (degrees; Canal 4, Midtown -4).
There is no `buzzard`, no `waveAdd` and no `waveAddOn` (removed 2026-11-28).

**`checks`** (checker inputs, the engine may ignore): `goonDoors[]`, `sightlines`, Canal `playerOnlyGaps[]`, Midtown
`shootThrough[]`. **`views`** (Midtown only; art camera list). **`colliderBudget`** (planned against placed clutter).
**`propPlacements.items[]`** (art side: catalogue name, position, rotationY, colliders). Engine needs only the indices
`colliders.boxes[].owner.index` refers to.

## 4. Counts the loader gate (`provides`) must use

Canal: 3 objectives, 38 ground, **0 air**, 18 explosives, 14 title keys. Midtown: 3, 30, **0 air**, 17, 14. Hold mode must
not require air spawns for these levels (progression.md 3.4). Files are in `tools/levels/data/`; they move to
`Content/Data/level_<id>.json` with E1 (`levels.json` entry per level, stage 3). The Block's files are untouched.
